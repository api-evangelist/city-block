# Cityblock Health

> Cityblock Health is a value-based healthcare provider delivering integrated primary care, behavioral health, and social-services support to Medicaid and lower-income (dual-eligible) Medicare populations across multiple U.S. markets. Cityblock pairs community-based care teams with a proprietary care-delivery technology platform to coordinate wraparound care for historically underserved communities. Backed by 8vc. Cityblock operates as a care provider and does not publish a public developer API surface.
