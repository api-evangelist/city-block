Author(s): Saad Khan , Senior Product Designer, Cityblock Health; and Stuart Kim , Product Design Lead, Cityblock Health Since 2017, Cityblock Health has been innovating ways that healthcare professionals work together to deliver care at scale to people that need it the most, primarily Medicaid and dually eligible individuals. These are marginalized populations that have very limited access to care. In many cases, Medicaid reimbursement does not always cover the full cost of care for these individuals.
