Author: Hope Tambala , Senior Software Engineer — Design Systems What led me to UX engineering I remember looking at paper health records in a house without electricity by candlelight one evening. My host dad (a military physician who was incredibly smart and committed to the health of his patients) asked me to help him organize some of his patients’ records for some vaccine deliveries he needed to do the next day. As a Peace Corps volunteer, you go into many situations during your service where you’re just dumbfounded by the situations you find yourself in.
