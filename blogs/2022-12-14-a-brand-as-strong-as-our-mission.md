Author(s): Caprice Yu, Executive Creative Director, Cityblock Health In September of this year, we celebrated our five-year anniversary at Cityblock. This was a huge moment; five years ago, a group of people passionate about equality in healthcare decided to radically improve the health of marginalized communities at scale. In the years that followed, over 1200 passionate people joined them and have, together, impacted the lives of tens of thousands of people in seven states.
